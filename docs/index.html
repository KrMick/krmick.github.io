隐私政策

LiveTrans · 更新日期：2026 年 11 月 26 日 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 26 日

LiveTrans 是一款 macOS 平台上的实时字幕与翻译工具。我们深知隐私的重要性，本政策说明本 App 如何处理你的信息。

核心承诺：所有语音识别与翻译均在你的 Mac 本机完成，音频与字幕文本不会上传到任何服务器。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

我们不收集任何个人信息。本 App 没有账号系统，不需要你提供姓名、邮箱、电话号码等身份信息，也不会上传你的音频、字幕内容或使用行为。

2. 本机数据处理

  • 音频：通过系统音频捕获或麦克风采集的声音，仅用于本机实时识别，处理完即丢弃，不存储、不上传。
  • 字幕文本：识别出的原文与翻译结果仅在本机内存和你的字幕历史中保留；你可将历史导出为本地文本文件，导出位置由你自行选择。
  • 设置：界面语言、字幕外观、音频来源等偏好设置保存在本机的 UserDefaults 中。
  • 购买状态：专业版解锁状态在本机 Keychain 中缓存，并依据 Apple StoreKit 的交易信息验证。

3. 权限使用

  • 语音识别：用于把音频转为文字，全部在本机进行。
  • 麦克风：仅当你选择麦克风作为音频来源时使用。
  • 系统音频捕获：仅当你选择电脑音频作为音频来源时使用，用于采集系统播放的声音。
  • 文件读写：仅在你主动导出字幕历史时，访问你选择保存的位置。

以上权限均可在 macOS 系统设置中随时撤销。

4. 第三方服务

本 App 不含任何第三方 SDK、广告或数据分析服务。应用内购买由 Apple App Store 处理，支付信息由 Apple 管理，我们无法接触你的支付信息。

5. 问题反馈

如果你通过 App 内的「问题反馈」发送邮件，邮件内容由你自行撰写并通过系统邮件应用发送，我们仅在收到后用于回复与改进，不会用于其他用途。

6. 儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

7. 政策更新

若本政策有重大变更，我们会在 App 内或本页面更新说明。继续使用本 App 即表示你接受更新后的政策。

8. 联系我们

如对隐私政策有任何疑问，请发送邮件至：service@cyjaf.com

Privacy Policy

LiveTrans · Updated: November 26, 2026 · Effective: November 26, 2026

LiveTrans is a real-time captioning and translation app for macOS. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Our commitment: All speech recognition and translation run entirely on your Mac. Audio and caption text are never uploaded to any server.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. LiveTrans has no account system and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any other identity information. Your audio, caption content, and usage are never uploaded.

2. On-Device Data Processing

  • Audio: Sound captured from system audio or your microphone is used only for on-device recognition. It is processed and discarded immediately — never stored or uploaded.
  • Caption text: Recognized original text and translations remain only in your device's memory and caption history. You may export the history to a local text file, at a location of your choosing.
  • Settings: Preferences such as interface language, caption appearance, and audio source are stored locally in UserDefaults.
  • Purchase status: Pro unlock status is cached in the local Keychain and verified against Apple StoreKit transaction information.

3. Permissions We Use

  • Speech Recognition: Used to convert audio to text, entirely on-device.
  • Microphone: Used only when you select the microphone as your audio source.
  • System Audio Capture: Used only when you select computer audio as your source, to capture sound playing on your Mac.
  • File Access: Used only when you choose to export caption history, and only to the location you select.

All of these permissions can be revoked at any time in macOS System Settings.

4. Third-Party Services

This app contains no third-party SDKs, ads, or analytics services. In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store; payment information is managed by Apple and is never accessible to us.

5. Feedback

If you send feedback using the in-app option, the email is composed by you and sent through your system mail app. We use it only to reply and improve the app, and for no other purpose.

6. Children's Privacy

LiveTrans is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the notice within the app or on this page. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please email: service@cyjaf.com
